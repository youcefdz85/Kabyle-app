Sunday, January 10, 2027 · 6:00 PM · Keswick Theatre, Glenside, PA · Tickets: axs.com/events/1696414/zedek-mouloud-tickets
Before anything: the ads run on the client's ad account and the client's card. Never share passwords: the client adds you as a partner/admin from her own Business account. Menus in Meta change often; if a button has a slightly different name, look for the closest option.

1Get access (with the client, 10 min)

  1. The client opens business.facebook.com (Meta Business Suite) and logs in.
  2. Settings → People → Add → enters your e-mail → gives you access to the Facebook Page, the Instagram account and the Ad account (full control on ads).
  3. You accept the invitation in your e-mail.
  4. In the ad account: Billing → Payment methods → the client adds her card.
  5. Check the ad account settings: time zone = Eastern Time, currency = USD.

2Prepare the ticket link

Use this link in every ad, so AXS traffic from the ads can be recognized:

https://www.axs.com/events/1696414/zedek-mouloud-tickets?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=zedek_philly_2027

Ask the promoter or AXS: can a Meta Pixel be added to the event page? If yes, you can later switch the campaign goal to Sales and see real ticket purchases in Ads Manager. If not, use the Traffic setup below and track sales weekly with the client.

For each ad

  1. Format: single video.
  2. Primary text, headline, description: paste from the copy file (Meta_Ads_Copy.txt).
  3. Call to action: Buy Tickets (or Book Now / Learn More if not available).
  4. Website URL: the link with UTM from step 2.
  5. Thumbnail: pick a frame with Zedek's face or the poster (not a black frame).
  6. Turn off "Multi-advertiser ads" and automatic music if offered.

6Before you click "Publish"

  • Ad account in the client's name, with her card
  • Date, time and venue correct in all text: Sunday, Jan 10, 2027 · 6 PM · Keswick Theatre, Glenside, PA
  • Link opens the right AXS page (tap it in the preview)
  • Budgets correct, end date Jan 10 at 5 PM
  • Videos play with sound in the preview; logo and text are not hidden by buttons
  • Music/footage rights confirmed in writing by the promoter
  • Client approved the ads (screenshot of the previews by WhatsApp)
Meta reviews the ads (usually a few hours). If one is rejected, read the reason in Ads Manager and fix the text: often a claim or a word in capitals is the problem.
