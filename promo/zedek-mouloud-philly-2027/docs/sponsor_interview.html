SPONSOR SPOTLIGHT · INTERVIEW GUIDE

Hanane's Signature Patisserie

1250 Bethlehem Pike #G, Hatfield, PA 19440 · Sponsor of Zedek Mouloud — Live in Philadelphia · Sunday, Jan 10, 2027 · Keswick Theatre
Story angle: "Hanane brings the taste of home to the community. Zedek Mouloud brings its sound. On January 10, we celebrate both."

● Gold number = must-get question if time is short  ·  Ask her to repeat the question in her answer ("I started Hanane's Signature because…")  ·  English first, one line in Kabyle at the end.

A. Her story

  1. Who are you, and what is Hanane's Signature Patisserie?
  2. Where did your love for pastry begin? Who taught you?
  3. Why did you open your bakery here, in Hatfield?
  4. What was the hardest moment in starting it, and what kept you going?

B. Her craft and heritage

  1. What makes your pastries different? What is your signature?
  2. Your customers are offered Algerian tea. Why is that tradition important to you?
  3. Which pastry reminds you most of home? Tell us its story.

C. Community

  1. What does your bakery mean to the Algerian and Kabyle community here?
  2. Who comes through your door? Is there a customer story that touched you?
  3. Why is it important to support community businesses and events?

D. The event

  1. Why did you decide to support Zedek Mouloud's concert?
  2. What does Zedek Mouloud's music mean to you or your family?
  3. What does Yennayer mean to you? How do you celebrate it?
  4. Will you prepare something special for Yennayer? (only if she wants to)
  5. Invitation: invite everyone to the concert, in your own words. Essentials: Sunday, January 10 · Keswick Theatre · tickets on AXS.com
  6. A closing line in Kabyle: "Aseggas ameggaz!"

E. Shoot checklist

Cameras
  • Sony A7 III on tripod: medium shot, eye level, 35–50 mm
  • 23.976 fps · shutter 1/50 (1/60 if the lamps flicker)
  • iPhone on tripod 2: close-up from ~45°, 4K 24 fps, exposure + focus locked
  • White balance set manually and fixed
Light
  • LED panel = key light, 45° to the side, slightly above the eyes, diffused
  • RGB light = warm amber / gold accent behind her (no blue or purple)
  • Window to her side, never behind her
Sound
  • Lavalier mic on her chest, cable hidden
  • Backup audio on the iPhone
  • Music, fridge hum and coffee machine off
  • One clap in front of both cameras before each answer
B-roll
  • Storefront and sign
  • Display case, slow sideways move
  • Close-ups: tarts, mille-feuille, croissants, fruit pastries
  • Tea poured from a height (slow motion 60/120 fps)
  • Her hands decorating or arranging pastries
  • Serving a customer (with their consent)
  • Pendant lamps, the welcoming room
  • Concert poster on the counter with the QR code
  • Her smiling, holding a pastry or the poster
  • To camera: "See you January 10!"
Ideas to suggest (her choice)
  • Poster + flyers with QR code at the register
  • A Yennayer pastry or tea special
  • Tag each other's posts the same day
After the shoot: share the best footage on Google Drive → 45–60 s sponsor spotlight Reel + 15 s version, in the campaign style (logo, poster, tickets on AXS.com).
